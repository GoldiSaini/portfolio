# GoldiSaini.com – Product Vision & Design System v1.0

## 1. Product Vision

### Mission

Create a premium engineering portfolio that positions Goldi Saini as a Quality Engineering Leader specializing in AI-powered test automation, automation architecture, and engineering excellence.

### Goal

When a recruiter, hiring manager, or engineering leader visits the website, they should immediately understand:

* This is an experienced engineering leader.
* This person designs systems, not just tests.
* This person communicates clearly.
* This person understands modern software engineering.
* This is someone I want to interview.

---

# 2. Success Criteria

A visitor should be able to answer these questions within 30 seconds:

* Who is Goldi?
* What problems does he solve?
* What technologies does he specialize in?
* What impact has he delivered?
* How can I contact him?

# 6. Design Principles

Every design decision should follow these principles:

1. Minimal over flashy.
2. Content over decoration.
3. Fast over complex.
4. Accessibility by default.
5. Mobile-first.
6. Consistent spacing and typography.
7. Every animation must have a purpose.

---

# 20. Definition of Done

The website is successful when:

* It loads in under two seconds.
* It works beautifully on mobile and desktop.
* It reflects senior engineering quality.
* Recruiters can understand the value proposition in under 30 seconds.
* Every page feels like part of one cohesive product.
* The codebase is clean enough that another engineer could easily maintain it.
